# Mission and product

## Mission

Let anyone advertise on digital city screens: a café, a creator, a local club, and later a person with something to say. Today that airtime is effectively reserved for brands with agency budgets. Programmatic buying changes that, because screens are sold a few seconds at a time through auctions, so a small budget can still buy real airtime. We build the buyer side of those auctions for people who have never bought outdoor ads.

We start in Dublin, then expand to the rest of Ireland, Northern Ireland and the UK. Pricing is in EUR, and Irish advertising rules come first.

## Who it's for

| Who | What they need from us |
|---|---|
| Small businesses | Footfall near the shop, a budget they choose, a creative made for them, a plain report |
| Creators and influencers | A launch or event seen on real screens, and photos to share |
| Local organisations | Awareness for an event on a set date |
| Individuals (after launch) | A personal message, with strict checks |

## What we're building

| Product | Used by | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Advertiser App | Customers | Sign up, plan a campaign on a map, make or upload a creative, pay into a wallet, launch, see results. It explains the bidding in plain words |
| Advertiser Agent | Customers | One AI agent per account. It plans, explains and runs campaigns within limits the customer sets, says it is an AI, and asks before spending |
| Campaign Manager | Everyone | The single gate to the bidder: accounts, campaigns, creatives, compliance, wallet, reports |
| DSP core | Machines | Compiles campaigns, bids on screens through SSPs in under 1ms, and records wins, plays and spend |
| Ops Console | Our team | Runs the company: approvals, screens, the compliance queue, money, system health. Staff work by chatting with agents |
| Central Brain and enterprise agents | Our team | The company's orchestrator. It knows the workflows, routes work to agents (campaign, compliance, creative, finance, supply, analytics, support, ops), keeps memory, asks humans for approval and logs everything |

We make money from a margin on the media bought through us, plus paid creative tools.

## What the prototype must prove

By Gate 1 (5 Feb 2027), a customer can go from sign-up to a campaign running on screens: simulated screens first, then an SSP sandbox. Compliance is checked, money is taken, our spend matches the SSP's, and results are reported. Agents do most of the work, and humans approve the risky steps.

Left out of the prototype: personal messages from individuals, shared slots, camera measurement, the mobile app, NI and the UK, and the industry agent-buying protocols.
